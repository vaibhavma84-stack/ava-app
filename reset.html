Reset the apps

For when AVA or the Library keeps opening an old version however many times it is closed and reopened.

Your entries are kept.

Certificates, sea time, notes, documents and the text read out of them are all in the database, and nothing on this page touches it. Only the downloaded code is thrown away — the phone fetches it again, current, on the next launch.

This will:

  • unregister both apps' service workers
  • empty the caches they downloaded into
  • reopen the app fresh

Do it with a connection. Without one there is nothing to fetch back, and the apps will be blank until there is.
